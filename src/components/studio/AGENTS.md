# Studio / XR rules
- Isolate drei Text, model and texture Suspense inside Canvas so loading never remounts the workspace. XR conditional groups have distinct keys and independently suspended labels.
- HandleStore alone owns gestures and two-pointer uniform scale/yaw; commit once, cancel interrupted tracking, protect locked parts. Head-pinch hysteresis and button dwell remain isolated assists.
- IWSDK hands on, anchors opt-in; one session/HandleStore; public/hands holds hand+controller profiles. IWER isn't hardware proof.
- Head-gaze pinch uses real primary-hand input, disables competing near pointers and never claims eye tracking.
- Primary-hand selection is explicit in accessibility settings; left hand defaults to near-transform assist, never palm-menu commands or teleport. Keep controls central, not palm-anchored.
- Furniture uses checked CC0 pointers, normalized bounds, cloned materials and isolated Suspense. Dining assets must be dining furniture, not stretched lounge/coffee models.
- Packed GLBs must match binary source data with finite accessor bounds; headers alone do not prove geometry integrity.
- src/lib/r3f-dev-source gives 3D objects a dev-only `data` target so the source-inspector's data-tsd-source tag cannot crash the canvas; keep it imported by Viewport.
- Thumbstick locomotion is an app-owned useFrame in Viewport using pure xr-input helpers (head-yaw walking, turning around the head, wall sliding); scene/walkthrough gates steps and teleport, both move XROrigin, and teleport uses the world hit since the origin can turn.
- XR board hides until boardVisible says needed. Catalog placement validates clearance with one undo snapshot.
- VR enters life-size; AR fits a confirmed table with no synthetic background and rolls back on denial; completed yaw/zoom gestures persist in scene undo/recovery.
- DesktopXRMouse is emulator-only (detected via IWER's runtime container, since emulators spoof the Quest UA): relays mouse to scene pointers, right-drag/arrow look, WASD walk and Space jump by moving XROrigin around the head (shared look pitch lives in xrLook, applied via xr-look.applyLook, also used by right-stick up/down; A jumps); never mounts on real headsets.
- Desktop/phone part dragging uses useFloorDrag: pressing an unlocked part disables OrbitControls for that press and moves it over the surface under the pointer, wheel lifts, release drops (tap still selects); empty space and locked parts orbit. One gesture owns a press: presses on gizmo handles go to TransformControls (via activeGizmo), otherwise the gizmo is disabled for the drag; translate mode hides Y so moves stay on the floor; canvas touch-action is forced to none in CSS because control libraries reset it on unmount.
- VR smoothness: never toggle shadowMap.enabled or castShadow by quality (recompiles every material and freezes the headset); in VR shadows refresh on a quality-based timer. Scene subscribers select only the runtime fields they draw. XR pointers hit furniture box proxies; detailed meshes use pointerEvents "none". Emulated navigator.xr (no native XRSystem tag) caps canvas pixel ratio before entry.
- The Canvas root (Viewport) subscribes to scene primitives only and the Environment is a memoized child: re-rendering it recaptures the environment (PMREM), which stalls a frame and resets the XR drawing area, so every edit made the view jump.
- Released parts settle via src/lib/scene/settle (pure; settle-scene reads the store): VR/hand/gizmo releases "drop" onto the highest floor/table/desk/counter/shelf/box/bench top under the centre, floor drags "slide" and never climb; walls, windows and ceilings keep their height. AI add/move never sinks below ground.
- The main XR board holds Speak/Type command, Undo/Redo and Turn ±90°/Delete for the selection; XRKeyboard types AI commands with pokes, rays or a physical keyboard and submits through the normal reviewed proposal flow.
- VR grabs carry freely in 3D (users lift/lower at will), clamped only above the floor; every release "drops" onto the surface below. Emulator screen-mouse grabs and desktop/phone drags ride the surface under the pointer (settle.aimedSurface) plus a wheel/R-F lift (heldPart.lift). heldPart (lib/xr-held) lets the right stick or Q/E add 90° turns while held; the stick does not turn the user then. DesktopXRMouse must always deliver a release for a relayed press (panel, blur, cancel).
- Walking height: scene/walkthrough groundAt/standingHeight treat floors, flat wide slab/platform boxes (AI upper floors; never ceilings/roofs) and stair/step/tread/landing/ramp parts (not railings) as walkable; doors/lintels/headers never block and the walking body radius fits 0.8 m doorways with STEP_UP/MAX_DROP/HEADROOM limits; Viewport eases XROrigin y toward the ground under the head. Storeys are 2.7 m and the floor selector raises the origin by that amount.
